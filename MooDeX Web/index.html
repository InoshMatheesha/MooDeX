Features

Built for people with more games than free evenings.

Four things MooDeX does well, instead of forty it does badly.

Know exactly where your time went

MooDeX times every session automatically and rolls it up into a dashboard — total hours, your three most-played titles, and one game you crown as the all-time favourite. Nothing to log by hand.

  • Per-session and lifetime playtime
  • Automatic most-played ranking
  • Playing / Completed / Wishlist / Stopped counts

Launch anything from one shelf

Point MooDeX at any .exe — Steam, Epic, GOG, or a folder you unzipped years ago. It finds the matching cover art and launches the game directly, timing the session as it goes.

Find the next one

Trending, upcoming and popular releases pulled live from RAWG. Add anything that catches your eye straight to your library or wishlist.

Covers that actually match

Auto-matching gets most titles right on import. When it doesn't, search the RAWG catalogue and pick the correct cover yourself — then edit the rating, status and your own notes in the same dialog.

The app

Five screens. No menus buried three levels deep.

Dashboard

Your library summarised the moment you open the app: hours played, status counts, most-played podium, and the last game you touched.

My Library

Everything you own or want, with a status, a rating out of ten, and a notes field for the things you'd otherwise forget by next weekend.

PC Games

Imported executables, matched to real artwork and launchable in one click. Sessions are timed from launch to exit without an overlay.

Discover

Trending, upcoming and popular releases straight from the RAWG database. One click files anything into your library or wishlist.

Covers & details

Search the catalogue, pick the right cover, and set rating, status and notes in a single dialog — no round trip through three different screens.

How it works

Set up once. Then forget it's running.

  1. 01

    Add your games

    Import any executable from your drive, or add titles straight from the RAWG catalogue. Artwork and metadata are filled in for you.

  2. 02

    Play as you normally would

    Launch from MooDeX and it times the session in the background. No overlay, no injected DLLs, nothing sitting on top of your game.

  3. 03

    Watch the picture build

    Hours, rankings and completion status accumulate on their own, so your library tells you something true a few months in.

Under the hood

A real Windows app, not a website in a box.

MooDeX is written in C# on .NET 10 with WPF and the MVVM pattern. It starts fast, idles at almost nothing, and keeps your library in plain JSON on your own machine — so nothing is trapped if you ever move on.
