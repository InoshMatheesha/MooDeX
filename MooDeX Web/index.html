Core Features

Everything your library needed, in one native app.

Four integrated systems keep your collection organized, your stats accurate, and your next game one click away.

Dashboard & Playtime Stats

Live playtime tracking, your top 3 most-played titles ranked by session count, and a G.O.A.T selector that crowns your all-time favorite — updated in real time.

Playtime tracking Top 3 played G.O.A.T selector

One-Click Game Launcher

Import any .exe from your PC, let MooDeX auto-match cover art via RAWG, and launch straight from your library. Playtime is tracked automatically in the background.

.exe import RAWG cover match Auto playtime

Discover Games

Explore Trending, Upcoming, and Popular titles pulled live from the RAWG video game database. Add any title to your library or wishlist instantly with one click.

Trending Upcoming Popular

Seamless Auto-Updater

MooDeX checks GitHub Releases automatically in the background. When a new version is available, a single click downloads and installs the update — no manual steps required.
