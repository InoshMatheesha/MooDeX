FAQ

Questions, answered.

Something else? Open an issue on GitHub.

Windows says "Windows protected your PC". Is it safe?

The installer is not signed with a paid code-signing certificate yet, so SmartScreen does not recognise it. Choose More info, then Run anyway. The source is public and every release lists SHA-256 checksums.

Does it work with Steam, Epic and Ubisoft games?

Yes. Launch them through their store or through MooDeX: MooDeX recognises the real game executable by its exact path and records the whole session.

Where is my data stored?

In one database under %LOCALAPPDATA%\MooDeX\v2, with a rolling daily backup. You can export it to JSON at any time.

What is sent over the internet?

Only cover downloads, game lookups when you search, and the update check. No account, no analytics, no telemetry.

Why does Discover ask for a RAWG key?

Covers and game details come from the RAWG catalogue. Add a free personal key in Settings; it is kept in Windows Credential Manager. Your library works fine without it.

I used MooDeX 1. Do I keep my playtime?

Yes. Settings includes an importer for your old library. You review everything before it is applied, and your old file is backed up first.
