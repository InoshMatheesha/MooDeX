Features

Small app. Serious about the details.

Playtime that is actually right

Follows the hand-off from Steam, Epic and other launchers to the real game, and keeps timing until it closes.

Point at a folder. Done.

Finds each game's real executable, matches cover art and watches the folder for new installs.

Play straight from the card

Hit play on a card or in the tray menu. Launch from anywhere else and the session is still recorded.

One-click updates

Install new versions from Settings. Every package is signature-checked before it runs.

Private by design

One local SQLite database with daily backups and JSON export. No accounts, no analytics, no telemetry.

Light, native, quick

Written in Rust with Tauri 2. Starts fast, sits in the tray and stays out of your games.

Up and running in three steps.

  1. 1

    Install

    Run the 5 MB installer. No account, nothing to sign up for.

  2. 2

    Add your games

    Point at your game folders, search the catalogue or import MooDeX 1.

  3. 3

    Play

    Launch from MooDeX, the tray or anywhere else. Every session is recorded.
